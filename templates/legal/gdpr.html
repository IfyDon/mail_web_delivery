Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your experience.

Categories we use

Category Purpose Can opt out?
Strictly Necessary Session management, CSRF protection, authentication. No — required for the service to function.
Performance Anonymised analytics on page load times and error rates to improve reliability. Yes
Functional Remembering your preferences (theme, language, dashboard layout). Yes
Targeting Measuring effectiveness of marketing campaigns (no third-party ad networks). Yes

Managing your preferences

You can update your cookie preferences at any time using the button below. Your choice is stored in localStorage under the key wm_cookie_consent and is never sent to our servers.

Browser controls

You can also manage or delete cookies via your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent login from working correctly.
